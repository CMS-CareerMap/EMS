How CareerMap Solutions starts using the Employee Management System, step by step · 8 October 2026

EMS is at: https://ems.careermapsolutions.in — on a computer or a phone.

This guide takes you from the first sign-in to the first payroll. Each step says who does it: the Super Admin, HR or Accounts. Do the steps in order. Steps 1 to 5 take about half a day; most of it is typing in your own data.

Step 1 — Sign in, and change the first password Super Admin

  1. Open https://ems.careermapsolutions.in and sign in with the administrator login.
  2. Top right, your name → My Profile → Password. Choose a new password of at least 10 characters.

This administrator login belongs to no employee. Keep it safe, for emergencies. The owner gets a login of their own in step 3.

Step 2 — The company's settings Super Admin

Everything is under Settings (left menu). Most settings already have sensible values: change only what is different for you.

Settings tabWhat to fill in or check
CompanyThe company's legal name, address, phone and HR email. Under Attendance Geofence: the office's location and how far from it a check-in is allowed (50 metres works well).
OrganisationYour Departments, Designations and Shifts (each shift's hours, grace period and half-day rules).

Users & Roles → PasswordsWho sets passwords. To start: HR sets employees' passwords and the Super Admin sets those of people with a role. Nobody can change their own, except the Super Admin.
NotificationsWhich notices are sent. Every notice appears in the app's bell.

Step 3 — The owner Super Admin

  1. Employees → Add Employee: the owner's details. Leave "Give them a login" unticked here.
  2. Open the owner's page → Logins → Add login. Role: Super Admin; the owner's own work email; Add login.
  3. EMS shows a one-time link. Copy it and send it to the owner. They open it and choose their own password. A Super Admin's password is always their own.
  4. Once they have chosen it: Settings → Company Tree → Make owner next to the owner's name.

From now on the owner signs in with their own login. Their own leave and requests are recorded directly, with nobody's approval.

Step 4 — People with a role: HR, Accounts, managers Super Admin

For each of them, Employees → Add Employee:

  1. Fill in their details and their Reporting Manager. For somebody already working here, also Confirmed On: the date their probation ended. Left empty, EMS treats them as a new joiner, in onboarding and then on probation.
  2. Tick Give them a login to the system. Choose their Role, type their work email, and type a password twice.
  3. Add Employee. The login works at once. Tell the person their password yourself; it is not shown again.

Somebody with a role also needs an employee login for their own attendance, leave and payslips: their page → Logins → Add another login → Role Employee. The email is optional. Without one, they sign in with their Employee ID.

Step 5 — Everybody else HR

Many at once, from a spreadsheet:

  1. Employees → Import → Download a template. Fill it in: one row per employee. For people already working here, fill in confirmed_on too (the date their probation ended); left empty, they start as new joiners.
  2. Upload it. EMS checks every row first and names any problem with its line number. Nothing is saved until every row is right.
  3. Import. Employees with an email get a login that waits for its password ("No password yet"). Those without an email get no login yet: give one on their page → Logins → Add login, Role Employee, no email needed, and a password.

One at a time: Employees → Add Employee, tick Give them a login, keep the role Employee, and type a password twice. The email is optional. For somebody already working here, fill in Confirmed On.

Passwords: for each login that waits, Settings → Users & Roles → the key button next to "No password yet". You can also open the person's page → Logins → Set password. Tell each person:

Sign in at https://ems.careermapsolutions.in with your Employee ID (capital or small letters both work) or your email, and the password HR gave you. If you forget it, ask HR for a new one.

Who reports to whom Super Admin: Settings → Company Tree → place each person under their manager. Leave and requests go to that person.

EverybodyAttendance: Check In and Check Out on Home, from a phone at the office. If you use a biometric machine, HR imports its file: Attendance → Import.

Step 7 — The first payroll, at month end Accounts Super Admin

  1. Accounts: Payroll → Payroll runs → the month → Run payroll. This makes a draft: check the payslips. After any correction, press Recalculate.
  2. Super Admin: the same month → Approve. Accounts prepares the payroll; only the Super Admin approves it.
  3. Accounts: Bank file → Download CSV, and upload it to the bank.
  4. Accounts: once the bank has paid, Mark as paid. Everybody then sees their payslip under Payslips, as a PDF.

Until a month is marked as paid, and before the lock day set in Payroll Config, the Super Admin can reopen it to correct a mistake. Once it is paid, it cannot be changed.

Every day after that

  • Employees check in and out on Home, apply for leave, and send requests (attendance corrections, work from home, overtime) from Requests.
  • Managers approve or reject from Waiting for you on Home.
  • HR sees the whole company's attendance and leave, and sets passwords when somebody forgets theirs.
  • Backups are taken by themselves every night, and checked every month. The Super Admin can see them in Settings → Audit Log → System jobs.
Need help? Forgotten passwords: employees ask HR; people with a role ask the Super Admin. For anything else, or anything that looks wrong, contact us. Tell us the page, what you clicked, and what you saw — a screenshot helps.
