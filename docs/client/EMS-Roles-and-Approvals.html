EMS — Roles and Approvals

How roles, reporting and approvals work in the CareerMap Solutions Employee Management System · 6 October 2026

This document explains, in simple words, how EMS handles roles, reporting and approvals.

It is built the way you described:
  • everybody works under somebody,
  • the person above approves,
  • and the Super Admin controls all of it.

2. Everybody works under somebody

Every person in the company reports to one person. The Super Admin decides who reports to whom. People with a role, like HR, Accounts and managers, are part of this too.

  • Only one person is marked as the owner. Somebody whose manager has simply not been set yet is not treated as the owner.
  • Nobody gets missed. If somebody has nobody above them, they show on a list for the Super Admin. Until the Super Admin places them, the Super Admin handles their requests.
  • The chart cannot go in a loop. EMS stops “A reports to B” if B already reports to A.
  • Only the Super Admin moves people. When HR adds a new joiner, HR chooses who they report to. After that, any change is made by the Super Admin, so nobody can take over somebody else's approvals by moving them.
  • Moving people is safe. If somebody gets a new manager, their waiting requests go to the new manager, who is told. If a manager leaves the company, their team shows on the Super Admin's list until they are placed under somebody else. If a manager's login is turned off, their team's requests go to the Super Admin.

  • HR does not approve leave. HR can see all leave requests, give everybody their yearly leave, and correct a balance when needed.
  • Any person with people under them approves their leave, whatever their role. For example, the Accounts Head approves the accountant's leave.
  • Who approves when there is no manager, who stands in for the manager (the Super Admin, the manager's own manager, or nobody), and who can cancel approved leave can all be changed later by the Super Admin.

4. Nobody does their own work

The rule. A person who does HR, Accounts or Admin work can never do that work for themselves. People at the same level or below cannot do it for them either. Only the people above them can.

If the person directly above cannot do that kind of work, it goes up to the next person who can, and finally to the Super Admin. Nothing gets stuck. The owner's own items need nobody: they are recorded directly.

5. Two logins for people with a role

A person with a role (HR, Accounts, Admin, Manager or Reporting Manager) gets two logins:

LoginWhat it is for
Employee loginTheir own things only: punch in and out, see their attendance, apply for leave, upload their documents, download their payslips, give their bank account.
Role loginThe work of their role, for example HR work or Accounts work. Approving their team's leave is done here too.
  • Both logins belong to the same person. The Super Admin adds the role login on that person's page (Employees → the person → Logins). This is how EMS knows it is the same person, so the rule in section 4 works from both logins.
  • Each login has its own password. A role login needs its own email address. An employee login does not need an email: the person signs in with their Employee ID.
  • Signing in: with the email, or with the Employee ID. Capital or small letters both work. For somebody with two logins, the Employee ID opens the employee login, and the role login signs in with its own email.
  • The top of the screen always shows which login is open, for example “Signed in as HR” or “Signed in as Employee”. On a phone it shows the role as a small label.
  • The Super Admin can turn one login off; the other keeps working. When somebody leaves the company, both logins are closed together, and they stay closed.
  • A login added by mistake, for example with a wrongly typed email, can be withdrawn before anybody uses it, and then added again correctly.
  • Normal employees have just one login.
Passwords.
  • HR sets employees' passwords. When HR adds an employee, HR types their Employee ID and a password, and tells the employee. The employee can sign in at once. Nothing is made up by EMS.
  • The Super Admin sets role logins' passwords (HR, Accounts, managers). HR cannot set them.
  • Nobody changes their own password, except the Super Admin. Somebody who forgets it asks HR, or the Super Admin for a role login. The sign-in page and My Profile say so.
  • A new password signs out the old one. When HR sets a new password, every phone or computer signed in with the old one is signed out, and the person is told in the app.
  • Employees added from a spreadsheet with an email get a login without a password. They show as “No password yet” until HR sets one.
  • A password must be at least 10 characters. Nobody, not even the Super Admin, can read a password once it is set.
  • The Super Admin can change these choices in Settings → Users & Roles → Passwords: for example, let employees set and change their own password from a one-time link, or ask for longer passwords.

7. The Super Admin controls roles

The Super Admin has a Roles & Permissions screen in Settings. From there, the Super Admin can:

  • Create a new role with any name, for example “Finance Head” or “Team Lead”.
  • Decide what each role can do by ticking boxes, grouped by area: Employees, Salaries, Attendance, Leave, Payroll, Payslips, Documents, Reports, and Settings and users.
  • Decide whose information each role can see, for employee records, salaries, attendance, leave, documents and payslips:
ChoiceThe role can see
Only their ownOnly their own information
Their teamThe people directly under them
Everybody under themEverybody below them, at every level
Their departmentEverybody in their department
Whole company, except seniorsEverybody, except the people above them. Useful for salaries
Whole companyEverybody

Payslips offer “Only their own” or “Whole company”. Payroll and Reports always show the whole company, every salary included, so give them only to roles that should see every salary.

  • Give any role to any person, in Settings → Users & Roles. A second login is added on the person's page (section 5).
  • Set who reports to whom on the Company Tree screen, and the approval choices on the Approvals screen.
  • Set the order of roles, for example HR comes under the Super Admin. A person can only give roles that are below their own.
  • Changes work straight away. Nobody needs to log in again. Before saving, the screen shows what the role will be able to do.
  • “Reset to default” puts one of the seven starting roles back to how it started.
Built-in safety. These are always on:
  • The Super Admin role cannot be changed or deleted, and there is always at least one Super Admin.
  • Nobody can change a role they have themselves.
  • Nobody can give somebody a role that would let that person see more than they can see themselves.
  • Nobody can set the password of, or turn off, the login of somebody above them in the company tree.
  • The seven starting roles cannot be deleted, only changed or reset. A role that somebody still has, or that other roles come under, cannot be deleted.
  • EMS warns if one role could both prepare and approve the payroll.
  • Every change is saved in the audit log: who changed what, and when.

  • HR does not accept resignations. The person the employee reports to does, as with leave. That person, or HR, can also call a resignation off if the employee decides to stay.
  • People already working here when EMS starts are added as Confirmed, with the date they were confirmed.
  • HR's own steps, like confirming or promoting an HR person, are done by the person above them (section 4). HR also cannot change the record of anybody above them in the company tree; the Super Admin does that.
  • A resignation stays private until it is accepted. Only the employee, HR, their reporting manager and the Super Admin can see it. An HR person who works under that employee does not see it, and when an HR person resigns, the other HR people do not see it either. Everybody else sees it once it is accepted.
  • Somebody who was to join but does not come: HR closes their record. No working day is recorded for them.
  • The dashboard helps. HR sees who is joining, who is still being onboarded, whose probation ends within 30 days, who has resigned and who is past their last day. A manager sees the resignations waiting for them.
  • The employee is told about every step, and every step is saved in the audit log.
  • The Super Admin can change the probation length and the notice period in Settings → Employee Lifecycle.

10. Requests, shifts and pay extras

Besides leave, employees ask for other things in one place: Requests. Each kind goes to one person, who approves or rejects it. A rejection always says why.

Work from home / On dutyDays away from the office (home, a client visit, travel). On those days they check in without the office location.Their reporting manager
OvertimeTime worked past the shift. Only overtime that is approved is paid, with that month's salary.Their reporting manager
Leave encashmentUnused leave turned into pay, for leave types that allow it.HR
Profile changeA change to their own phone, address, emergency contact and other personal details.HR
  • The Super Admin chooses who decides each kind (the reporting manager or HR) in Settings → Approvals, and whether working from home still needs a location reading.
  • Shifts have rules. For each shift: a grace period, when coming late or leaving early makes it a half day, the hours a full day and a half day need, and when overtime starts (after 30 minutes past the shift, to start). Late arrivals and overtime show on the attendance screen and in the reports.
  • Overtime pay is off until you turn it on in Settings → Payroll Config, so nobody is paid overtime by surprise. Once on, it is twice an hour's pay to start; the rate, and whether it is worked out on the full salary or on Basic and DA, are set there too.
  • Each leave type has its own rules: days of notice, the most days in one go, who can take it (for example, maternity leave for women), when a new joiner can use it, whether it is earned all at once or a little each month, half days (first half or second half), and whether it can be encashed and how many days a year. All of these are off until HR sets them.
  • Salary parts: Accounts can add new earnings or deductions (a Site Allowance, a Bonus) and choose whether each counts for PF, ESI and professional tax. Bonus and Commission are there to start.
  • Loans and salary advances: Accounts records the amount and how much to take back each month. It comes off the payslip every month until it is paid back.
  • Email: every notice can also go by email, once the company's email account is connected to EMS. The Super Admin can keep any notice to the app only.
  • Reports show each person only what their role lets them see. HR does not see payroll reports.

11. Other safety rules

  • Everything important is recorded. Sign-ins, role changes, salary changes, payroll steps, documents opened and files downloaded all go into the audit log. Nobody can edit or delete it.
  • Passwords are set by HR and the Super Admin (section 5). There are no shared or default passwords, and EMS never makes one up.
  • Yearly leave is given in one step. New joiners get leave for the months left in the year. People who have left get none. Unused leave carries forward, up to the limit set for each leave type.
  • Payroll is approved by the Super Admin. Accounts prepares it; only the Super Admin approves it. Once approved, that month cannot be changed.
  • PF and the Labour Codes. The new labour codes say PF must be worked out on at least half of the pay. EMS has a switch for this in Settings → Payroll Config, off to start. When your accountant confirms it applies, the Super Admin turns it on. The share (50%) can be changed if the government announces a new one. It applies from the next month's payroll. The Super Admin also chooses there which parts of the salary count for PF (Accounts can set it too, under Payroll → Components) (Basic and DA to start; Special Allowance too, if your accountant says so). HR records on each employee's record whether they are in the pension scheme (EPS), from their PF record, so these settings never change it.
  • Bank accounts are checked before any salary is paid into them (a setting, on to start). An account an employee sends in needs a photo of a cancelled cheque or passbook page.
